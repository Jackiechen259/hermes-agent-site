Privacy Policy

Hermes Personal Assistant · Last updated 3 November 2026

This application ("Hermes") is a private, single-user tool operated by its owner for the owner's own Google account. It is not offered as a service to the public, and it has no users other than its owner. This policy explains what happens to Google user data that the application accesses.

1. Data accessed

When the owner authorizes the application, it may access the following data belonging to the owner:

  • Gmail messages, threads and labels (read, compose, and modify)
  • Google Calendar events and calendars (read and write)
  • Google Drive files created by or shared with the owner
  • Google Sheets spreadsheets and Google Docs documents
  • Google Contacts (read-only)

2. How the data is used

Solely to carry out tasks the owner requests — for example reading an email, drafting a reply, adding a calendar event, or editing a document. The data is processed on the owner's own hardware. It is never used for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose the owner did not ask for.

3. Storage and security

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's private server, in files readable only by the owner's account.
  • Message, event and document content is held transiently in memory to complete a task. Content is not retained in any database, index, or backup beyond what the owner's own Google account already contains.
  • All traffic to Google APIs uses HTTPS.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is never sold, rented, traded, or transferred to any third party, advertising network, data broker, or information reseller. It is not disclosed to anyone except:

  • with the owner's explicit consent;
  • when required to comply with applicable law; or
  • to protect against security threats or abuse.

No human other than the owner reads the data. Automated processing is performed by AI models used as the assistant's reasoning engine; those calls carry only the minimum content needed for the task at hand.

Limited Use disclosure.
Hermes's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in the application's interface; it is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with applicable laws, or as part of a merger or acquisition; it is not used for serving advertisements; and it is not used to train or improve generalized or foundation AI/ML models.

5. Retention and deletion

The application keeps no independent copy of Google user data. Deleting content in the owner's Google account removes it from the application's reach immediately. Authorization can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which invalidates the stored tokens.

6. Children

The application is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than its owner.

7. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
